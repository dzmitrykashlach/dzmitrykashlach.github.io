## The project

The customer supplied the original artwork,
which was prepared for CNC machining and converted
into toolpaths.

The sign was machined from solid oak and then
hand-sanded and finished with natural oil.

## CNC process

The machining strategy used separate operations
for roughing, finishing and detail carving.

The final result combines CNC precision with
manual finishing.
